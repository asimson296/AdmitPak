Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. What We Collect

When you use AdmitPak, we may collect:

  • Account information — your email address and a securely hashed password when you sign up.
  • Tracked universities — the list of universities you choose to track.
  • Feedback — any message, category, or optional email you submit via our feedback form.
  • Basic technical data — your IP address (for abuse prevention) and browser type.

2. How We Use It

  • To provide the service (tracking universities, showing relevant admissions information).
  • To improve AdmitPak based on your feedback.
  • To prevent abuse and spam.

3. What We Don't Do

  • We never sell your data to anyone.
  • We never share your email with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not store passwords in plain text — they are hashed with bcrypt.

4. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Cookies

We use one cookie (admitpak_token) to keep you logged in. It expires after 7 days.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via the feedback button on any page.
