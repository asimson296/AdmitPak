GIKI

GIKI Admission Guide 2027 — Everything You Need to Know

By AdmitPak · Updated September 2026 · 5 min read

GIKI (Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology) is one of Pakistan's top three engineering universities — alongside NUST and FAST. Located in Topi, Swabi (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa), it has a residential campus, small batch sizes, and a strong reputation for producing CEOs, tech leaders, and entrepreneurs.

Why GIKI?

  • Top 3 in Pakistan for Engineering
  • Notable alumni at Microsoft, Apple, Tesla, AWS, and CEOs of major companies
  • Residential campus — full student life experience
  • Merit-based admission — entry test carries 85% weightage, no hidden quotas
  • Strong scholarship program — 16 different scholarship options

Eligibility Criteria

For Engineering Programs (EE, ME, ChemE, Civil, Materials, Computer Engg)

  • HSSC Pre-Engineering (Math, Physics, Chemistry) with 60%+ in Math, Physics, and overall
  • OR ICS (Math, Physics, CS) with 60%+ in Math, Physics, overall
  • OR Pre-Medical with Additional Math + 60%+ in Math, Physics, overall
  • OR A-Levels in Math, Physics, Chemistry with D or above in Math & Physics
  • OR DAE in relevant technology with 60%+

For Computing Programs (CS, SE, AI, Cyber, DS)

  • HSSC with Math, Physics, and any third elective with 60%+ overall and in Math, Physics
  • OR A-Levels in Math, Physics, and any third subject

For BS Management Sciences

  • HSSC in any stream (Pre-Engg, General Science, ICS, Pre-Medical, Humanities) with 60%+ overall

Entry Test & Merit Calculation

GIKI's admission test is the single most important factor — it carries 85% of the merit weightage:

ComponentWeightage
GIKI Admission Test85%
SSC / Matric / Equivalent15%

The test has two versions:

  • Engineering/Computing Exam — for all engineering, CS, AI, cyber, DS, SE programs
  • Management Exam — for BS Management Sciences

Application Fee

  • Rs. 3,500 for single test
  • Rs. 5,000 for both tests (if applying to both Engineering and Management)
  • Rs. 1,000 extra if applying for Financial Aid

Fee Structure (2025–26 rates)

ItemAmount
Admission Fee (one-time)Rs. 75,000
Security Deposit (refundable)Rs. 40,000
Semester Fee — Engineering & ComputingRs. 470,000
Semester Fee — Management SciencesRs. 412,500

Note: GIKI's semester fee includes accommodation charges. It's a residential campus — most students live on campus.

Scholarships — 16 Options

GIKI has one of the strongest financial aid programs in Pakistan:

Internal

  • GIK Merit Scholarship — top 18 positions in Engineering test, top 2 in Management. 20 seats. Fully funded.
  • GIK Financial Assistance for Top Female — top 7 females in Engineering, top 3 in Management. 10 seats. Fully funded.
  • GIK Financial Assistance (Need-cum-Merit) — 40 seats. Partial or full funding.
  • GIK Alumni Association — 30 seats (2nd year onwards).
  • Interest-Free Loan — for needy students, repayable in easy installments after graduation.

External (provincial + donor)

  • CMEEF (KPK), CM-Honhaar (Punjab), Sindh Scholarship, PEEF, KPEF
  • DWP + PSSF, Descon, Ihsan Trust, Lucky Cement, PEL, ICI, Habbah Trust

Application Process

  1. Apply online at admissions.giki.edu.pk
  2. Pay application fee (Rs. 3,500 or Rs. 5,000)
  3. Appear in the GIKI Admission Test (June–July 2027)
  4. Merit list published around 21 July 2027
  5. Pay admission dues to lock in your seat

Is GIKI Right for You?

If you want a world-class engineering education with a residential campus experience and don't mind living in a smaller town (Topi, Swabi), GIKI is an excellent choice. Its alumni network alone is worth it — GIKI grads are everywhere in top global companies.

The main downsides: it's more expensive than public universities, and location is remote. But for the right student, it's worth every rupee.
