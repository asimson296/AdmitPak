FAST-NUCES Fee Structure 2027 — Complete Breakdown

By AdmitPak · Updated September 2026 · 4 min read

FAST-NUCES is one of Pakistan's top universities for computing, engineering and business. But before you apply, you need to know the real cost. Here's the complete breakdown of FAST fees for the 2026–27 cycle, structured so you can plan ahead.

One-Time Fees (Payable at Admission)

ItemAmount
Admission FeeRs. 30,000
Security Deposit (refundable)Rs. 20,000
Applicant Admission FeeRs. 3,000

These are paid once at the time of admission. Security deposit is refunded when you complete your degree or leave the university.

Tuition Fee — Per Credit Hour

FAST charges tuition on a per-credit-hour basis. For the 2026–27 academic year:

Program GroupFee per Credit Hour
BBA / BSRs. 12,000

This is the same rate for both Business and Computing/Engineering programs at the undergraduate level.

How Semester Fee is Calculated

A typical undergraduate semester involves 15–18 credit hours. Here's how it works out:

  • 15 credit hours × Rs. 12,000 = Rs. 180,000
  • 18 credit hours × Rs. 12,000 = Rs. 216,000
  • Plus Student Activities Fund: Rs. 2,500
Estimated semester fee: Rs. 180,000 – Rs. 216,000 per semester, depending on your course load.

Other Charges

  • Student Activities Fund — Rs. 2,500 per semester (mandatory)
  • Hostel — separate charges if you opt for it
  • Miscellaneous — transcript fee, campus transfer, etc. (as needed)

Total Estimate — 4-Year BS Program

Over 8 semesters with 15 credits each:

  • Tuition: 15 × 12,000 × 8 = Rs. 1,440,000
  • Activities Fund: 2,500 × 8 = Rs. 20,000
  • One-time fees: Rs. 53,000
  • Total: ~Rs. 1,513,000 for 4 years

In practice, most students take 16–18 credits, so the actual total will be higher. Plan for Rs. 1.6 – 1.9 million for the full degree.

Financial Aid & Scholarships

FAST offers several scholarships to reduce this cost:

  • Merit Scholarship — full tuition for top 3 board position holders and top 3 in NU merit list
  • Study Loan — interest-free loan for bright, needy students
  • PEEF, Punjab Honhaar, OSAF, FANA, Ihsan Trust — external options

Is FAST Worth It?

FAST is more expensive than public universities like NUST or UET, but it has strong industry placement, especially in software engineering and business. For students aiming at careers in tech, the investment often pays off quickly. For students on tight budgets, FAST's scholarship program (especially the merit scholarship) can reduce the cost significantly.

Before applying, check FAST's official fee page for the latest rates — universities revise fees annually.
