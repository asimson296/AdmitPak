Pakistani University Admission Deadlines 2027

By AdmitPak · Updated September 2026 · 5 min read

One of the biggest mistakes Pakistani students make is missing deadlines. Each university has its own schedule — and if you're applying to 5–10 universities, tracking them all is a nightmare. This is the complete 2027 admission schedule for the top 8 universities.

Note: Dates are based on the last available official schedule. Universities update their 2027 schedules over time. Track any university on AdmitPak to get notified when dates are published.

NUST

  • NET Series 1 registration: 4 Oct – 14 Nov 2026
  • NET Series 1 test: 17 Nov 2026 onwards
  • Application window: Opens with NET Series 1
  • Application close: ~25 Jul 2027 (final series)

FAST-NUCES

  • 2027 schedule: Expected to open around Jan–Feb 2027
  • Last 2026 cycle: Applications ran May 20 – Jun 30
  • Test accepted: FAST Test / SAT / NTS NAT

LUMS

  • UG Online Application opens: ~1 Dec 2026
  • Deadline: 27 Jan 2027
  • LCAT test: 15 Feb 2027
  • Financial Aid deadline: 28 Feb 2027

COMSATS

  • 2027 schedule: Not yet published
  • Intakes: Fall (main) + Spring (some programs)
  • Entry test: NTS NAT-I

UET Lahore

  • 2027 applications: Expected to open around Sep–Oct 2027
  • Entry test: ECAT (compulsory for Engineering & Computing)
  • Merit formula: 17% Matric + 50% FSc + 33% ECAT

GIKI

  • 2027 applications: Expected Mar 2027
  • Application deadline: ~15 Jun 2027
  • GIKI Admission Test: Jul 2027
  • Merit list: ~21 Jul 2027

NED University

  • 2027 applications: Expected Mar–Apr 2027
  • Entry Test Phase 1: ~Jun 2027
  • Merit list: ~Jul 2027

Punjab University

  • Fall intake: Usually Sep–Oct
  • 2027 schedule: Not yet published
  • No entry test required — pure open merit

Final Advice

Apply to at least 5 universities. Pakistani admissions are competitive — even with great marks, you might not get your first choice. Spread your applications across top tier (NUST, FAST, LUMS), mid tier (COMSATS, GIKI, UET), and safe options.
